# Validation — hands-on competitor capture, round 1

## What this is
50 raw capture files across all 17 roster competitors, plus a generated index and the round's
running learnings. It executes ART-024. **It is evidence, not synthesis.** No claim here has been
through Gate A, and none should be quoted as a finding about the market without the caveats each
capture file carries.

## Why it lives in `artifacts/` and not `scratch/`
`scratch/` is gitignored. Checked with `git check-ignore` before moving. These files are the only
primary record of the round and would not survive a clone. The plan (ART-024 §4) staged captures in
`scratch/` on the assumption a human would move them into `research/sources/`; the operator is
denied write access there by `.claude/settings.json`, deliberately. Moving them into `artifacts/`
keeps them durable without crossing that boundary. **They are not registered as evidence-ledger
records and must not be** — CLAUDE.md forbids minting a ledger ID for a measurement.

## What was verified
- `CAPTURE-INDEX.json` is GENERATED by scanning the capture files. It is not hand-written and can be
  regenerated. Where it disagrees with a capture file, the capture file wins.
- All 50 files parse as valid JSON.
- 17 of 17 roster competitors have at least one capture.

## What is NOT verified
- **No competitor is complete.** Not one. Every capture file carries its own `not_observed` list.
- **Only one empty state** was captured in the entire round (Kayak's AI planner). Error, no-results
  and offline states are uncaptured everywhere.
- **Post-purchase and trip management are uncaptured on all 17** — they require a completed booking,
  which the operator may not make.
- **Tier-3's justification is unverified.** Four transport competitors captured; none observed
  completing an on-site transport purchase. Trainline's ticket fulfilment failed three attempts.
- **Business goal 5** — lounge and Fast Track pricing for an economy traveller — unresolved across
  all three airlines.
- Two competitors were blocked at cookie consent with no reject affordance (Omio, Tripadvisor).
  No accept was clicked on the client's behalf, so their results surfaces are uncaptured.

## Corrections this round made to its own findings
Six, all caught by capturing more rather than reasoning harder:
F-25 (effort axes measured on one vertical, stated as a market claim) · F-57 (Hopper sells the
thing nobody was said to own) · F-28 (per-vertical, not per-account) · F-75 (Kayak does both) ·
F-71 (AirCover is not shown at the decision) · and the AA findability sweep run in the wrong
language against a Spanish page.

## Method failures recorded rather than hidden
Eight instances of a result that looked like data and was not: five empty selector returns, two
404s that rendered with titles and headings, and one sweep in the wrong locale. Each is written
into the capture file where it occurred. The standing rule that came out of it — **an empty result
is a prompt to look, never a finding** — is now the round's most-used check.
