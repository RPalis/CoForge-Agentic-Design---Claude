14 competitors were confirmed in scope. This board renders how much of that scope the study actually reached — not what the six it reached found.

Read the ceiling before any number below

Restated from ART-012’s own opening section, because a dashboard is the easiest place in this system for a coverage ceiling to disappear into a clean grid.

  • 14 confirmed in scope · 6 profiled · 8 never profiled, and of those 8, 2 (Kayak, Skyscanner) carry evidence only in an earlier, superseded framing that may be cited as prior art and may never stand in for current coverage. Evidenced [ART-011 § Coverage], Evidenced [D-001], Evidenced [D-002].
  • 98 of 770 planned feature cells (12.7%) rest on Tier 1 live observation; 189 of 770 (24.5%) rest on any evidence at all. Evidenced [ART-011 § Evidence reached].
  • 37 of 112 planned journey stage-cells (33%) carry any rating. Evidenced [ART-011 § Evidence reached].
  • One of the five designated re-verification tests — the one the plan flags as supporting a headline opportunity — did not run. Evidenced [ART-011 § The untested claim].

What this board does not do

  1. It does not carry the market findings. Patterns, white space, opportunity scoring and the strategy are in ART-012 and are not re-rendered here; this board is scoped to coverage and evidence integrity, per brief. Reading those findings for prioritisation is a Phase 11 activity, not this one.
  2. It does not verify against a live competitor product. Every figure inherits ART-011/ART-012's desk study, dated 21 Jul 2026. Two profiled competitors document that they run display tests and may order results differently across sessions.
  3. It does not research the eight unprofiled competitors. Their row is blank by design (D-001); inventing a plausible figure for any of them would be exactly the defect that decision exists to prevent.
  4. It does not open research/sources/. Every number traces to ART-011 or ART-012 by section heading, never to the underlying [S-nn] register those artifacts declare — this board did not verify that register itself and does not reuse its notation.
  5. It does not judge the study. F-01/F-05 are reported as arithmetic facts with both figures shown; whether the recommendation should proceed while the transport-ticket test is unrun is one of the questions ART-011/ART-012 route to a human at Gate A, not a verdict this board renders.

Assumptions

  • A-1. ART-011’s recount (330 cells, two independent passes) is accepted as entered; this board did not re-derive it a third time from build_inventory.py, which this agent does not have standing to open (see “What this board does not do,” item 4).
  • A-2. The quintile bucketing of the ten cluster percentages into the five teal steps is this board’s own binning, not a source figure — it ranks the ten values against each other, not against a fixed external threshold. Every bar carries its exact percentage as a direct label, so no reading depends on which bucket a value fell into.
  • A-3. The row-state and tier classifications (profiled / prior-art-only / never; Tier 1 / Tier 2) are drawn from ART-011’s own table and scoreboard, not re-adjudicated here.
  • A-4. Colour values are read from token release 0.2.0, whose bone, ink and coral primitives carry a Gate A that a human has not yet cleared. If those values change, this board is regenerated rather than adjusted by hand.

Gaps carried forward from ART-011

The full register is 26 entries at ART-011 § Gaps. The three that would most change a reading of this specific board:

  • Eight competitors unprofiled — every convergence claim, every vacancy claim, and the transport-ticketing row depend on them.
  • The Phase 3 and Phase 9 cell totals do not reconcile (F-01/F-05) — this board substitutes the ART-011 recount throughout and says so at every use.
  • Source hashes were not recomputed by ART-011 (no shell held at that dispatch), so this board’s numbers are two steps removed from the raw bytes: ART-011 attests to the baseline manifest, and this board attests to ART-011.

Claim format

Per CLAUDE.md and ADR-017, every figure above is a measurement, inherited from ART-011/ART-012 and traceable to a named section in one of them. No [E-nnn] ID appears anywhere on this board and none is minted: research/evidence-ledger.json holds zero entries, no user was asked anything in this study, and no person is quoted. Citations use [ART-nnn § Section] only, against real headings in the two named artifacts — never the [S-nn] notation those artifacts declare, because this board did not open research/sources/ to verify it.
