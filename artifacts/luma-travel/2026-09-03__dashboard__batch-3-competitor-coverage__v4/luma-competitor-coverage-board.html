Meta — corrections from v3, what this board does not do, assumptions, claim format (click to expand; also fully present when printed)

Meta

Changed from v3 to v4

  • Unit charts, not bars or legends. The 770-cell cluster chart and the 112-cell journey grid are now dot/isotype matrices (ADR-021, dataviz layer). Absence is drawn as a hole — no fill, no border — never a different colour. Every colour-key legend is removed; row/column headers and per-cell mono labels carry the meaning directly.
  • Navigation. A sticky rail (scrollspy, deep-linkable anchors, two levels) replaces scroll-to-find. The headline stat travels with the rail so it never scrolls out of reach, without a second sticky bar competing with the grid's own headers on narrow viewports — tested at <64rem: only one element is ever sticky at a time.
  • Progressive disclosure. Every row and every one of the 112 journey cells is a click-to-pin trigger (never hover — unreachable on touch, invisible to keyboard) opening a side panel with full provenance, the grid staying visible and operable throughout. Two levels maximum; the panel never nests a third.
  • Prose. Cut hard; see validation.md for the measured before/after and the re-runnable command. Explanatory text that survives is annotation at point of use, not paragraphs of argument.
  • cf-chart-palette is not used anywhere — it is measured defective (C-036, ADR-021) and no chart on this board reaches for it.

Corrections to the v3/brief record, carried forward unchanged

Two, both load-bearing, both checked against the cited paper rather than taken on trust — reproduced verbatim from v3's own validation.md rather than re-litigated here.

  1. Song & Szafir (2018) supports the loud-blank design, but not with the margin a prior brief stated: the alpha-blended condition scored consistently high in one sub-study, an annotation condition (not removal) scored lowest in two of four, and accuracy was significantly affected by visualisation type in exactly one of four sub-studies. What survives: highlighting beat removal with the least qualification of any finding, and the mechanism is visual weight, not colour — which is what this board's full-opacity, full- footprint absence marks do.
  2. ART-012 gives ten cluster-level Unknown counts, not a 770-cell or 330-cell table of individual feature-row values per competitor. Building one would require inventing individual cell placements; forbidden. The isotype arrays below draw exact counts as anonymous unit icons (classic Isotype practice), never as invented individual placements. The one place true per-cell value data exists, complete, is the 8-stage×6-competitor journey table, extended to all fourteen by drawing the unprofiled rows blank rather than by inventing values for them.

What this board does not do

  • Does not carry the market findings, white space or opportunity scoring (ART-012's own job — Phase 11's job to interpret).
  • Does not research the eight unprofiled competitors. Drawing them as blank is not inventing a value; no capability rating is asserted for any of the eight, anywhere.
  • Has no filter, sort control or show/hide — a control that can conceal the eight blanks is the exact defect D-001 forbids. Sort order (worst-first in the cluster chart) is an editorial choice, stated, not a control.
  • Does not open research/sources/; every citation is [ART-nnn § Section].

Assumptions

  • A-1. ART-011's recount (330 cells, two passes) accepted as entered.
  • A-2. The cluster sort (worst-first) and the fourteen-row order (ART-011's own) are this board's editorial choices, stated rather than left to a control.
  • A-3. Row-state, tier and journey classifications are drawn from ART-011/012 verbatim. The eight unprofiled competitors' cells are drawn blank by construction (D-001), not because a value is stated for them.
  • A-4. Colour values are read from token release 0.2.0. bone/ink/coral carry a batch-level "APPROVED, 2026-09-02" note and per-token "not counted until a human approves" strings that disagree with each other — token-keeper's file to reconcile, not this board's call; values are treated as approved.
  • A-5. Per-cluster row counts for the full-14 denominators are read directly from ART-012 ("55 feature rows across ten clusters…"), not inferred by dividing a six-competitor count by six — the two methods agree exactly, cross-checked.

Claim format

Per CLAUDE.md/ADR-017, every figure is a measurement inherited from ART-011/ART-012, traceable to a named section. No [E-nnn] appears or is minted — the ledger holds zero records and no person is quoted in this study.
